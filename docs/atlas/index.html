THE ATLAS NOTEBOOK

Stories, sources
& a little uncertainty.

This atlas follows 34 divine figures across 25 story entries. The nine Muses and the Dioscuri are grouped; Heracles is included as a deified hero. Eighteen entries have a mapped birth, regional, arrival, or infancy tradition here; seven have no securely mapped location in the selected accounts. It is a curated introduction, not a complete catalogue of every ancient claim.

Reading the marks

Solid gold circles mark a birth tradition. Rust-colored rings mark a rival local claim. Diamonds mark a landing or a place where an infant was hidden. Pan’s Arcadian marker is regional. Coordinates are approximate modern aids to reading; they are not surveyed ancient birth sites.

The main view shows one selected tradition for each mapped god. Switch on “Rival claims & routes” to see additional mapped traditions and Aphrodite’s sea journey. A dashed line is a schematic narrative link, not an ancient travel route. “Unlocated” means the cited account supplies no fixed place; it does not mean no other tradition exists.
